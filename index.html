Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 May 2026. Developer: Luigi Iacopozzi — liacopozzi@gmail.com

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email address (Firebase Authentication, only if you create an account)
  • Vehicle data: fuel type, tank capacity, consumption, autonomy (stored in Firebase Firestore)
  • Refueling history: date, liters, price per liter, odometer reading, station name
  • Trip history: GPS coordinates, distance, start/end time
  • Preferences: language, route settings — stored locally on your device (AsyncStorage)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide fuel price comparison along your route
  • To calculate when and where to refuel based on your vehicle profile
  • To store your refueling and trip history in the cloud (if logged in)
  • We do not sell, share, or monetise your personal data

3. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google LLC): authentication and cloud data storage — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps Platform: route calculation and map display — Google Privacy Policy
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap: reverse geocoding (region detection) — no personal data transmitted
  • Fuel price APIs: MINETUR (Spain), data.economie.gouv.fr (France), public sources (Italy/Austria/Germany/Belgium) — no personal data transmitted

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained until you delete your account or uninstall the app. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Mi Perfil screen.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to: access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, and port your data. Contact: liacopozzi@gmail.com

6. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
